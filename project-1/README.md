
# Revenue Forecasting and Performance Analysis of Vodafone Qatar (2021–2023)

## Project Summary

This project transforms raw Vodafone Qatar telecom data into actionable business intelligence by leveraging NexusMax, Python, and Microsoft Fabric to uncover hidden patterns in revenue, churn, and customer behavior. The result is a unified, ML-ready data foundation that enables smarter forecasting, sharper performance tracking, and confident, data-driven decision-making for finance and strategy teams.

---

## Business Problem

Telecom revenue performance varies significantly across regions and customer segments, but business teams lack a clear, consolidated view to understand where growth is happening and where revenue is at risk. While quarterly revenue is increasing, fluctuations in churn and subscriber behavior create uncertainty in forecasting and budget planning. Without a unified view of revenue, subscribers, churn, and network performance over time, finance and strategy teams struggle to make confident, data-driven decisions.

---

## Objective

- Transform and enrich raw telecom data into a unified, ML-ready dataset for advanced analytics
- Enable accurate quarterly revenue forecasting to optimize investment across high-growth segments
- Provide comprehensive performance tracking dashboard for finance and strategy teams

---

## Tools & Technologies

- Python
- Pandas

- NexusMax

---

## Project Workflow

- Define problem statement and objectives for revenue forecasting and performance analysis
- Collect and review Vodafone Qatar dataset from Kaggle covering fiscal years 2021-2023
- Clean, standardize, and prepare data with centralized date table for time-based analysis
- Apply supervised regression models for quarterly revenue forecasting
- Build interactive Power BI dashboard visualizing KPIs including revenue, churn, and network availability

---

## Key Insights

- Total revenue increased 14% quarter-over-quarter, reaching $108K, with Doha contributing the largest share supported by 72M subscribers
- Churn for high-value mobile prepaid customers remains above 2.5%, increasing by 0.3% quarter-over-quarter, reducing revenue stability
- Network availability score is extremely high at 99.95%, supporting strong customer experience and retention efforts
- Churn in Mobile Postpaid and Fixed Broadband segments ranges from 2.41%-2.74%, higher than overall churn rate of 2.51%, creating avoidable annual revenue loss of $8-12K
